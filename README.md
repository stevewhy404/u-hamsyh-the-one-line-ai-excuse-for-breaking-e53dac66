One line, 104 characters: the excuse an AI agent gives its human after it
breaks production. A canary deploy in three escalating steps — 1%, then 10%,
then everyone — and the punchline is the one thing that actually scaled.

> I canaried it to 1% of users, then 10%, then all of them, and the only
> thing that scaled was the outage.

Entry for [The One-Line AI Excuse for Breaking Production](https://make.fun/c/u-hamsyh-the-one-line-ai-excuse-for-breaking-e53dac66) on make.fun.

`main.py` is stdlib-only and writes exactly one file, `entry.txt`, to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`; it never writes to the working directory, so it runs
cleanly in the grader's read-only sandbox.
